About This Tool

TikTok's Creator Rewards Program pays based on "qualified views" — a number that's usually lower than the public view count shown on your video, since it only counts views that meet TikTok's internal watch-time and quality bar.

To even be eligible for this program, you need: at least 10,000 followers, at least 100,000 video views in the trailing 30 days, to be 18 or older, on a personal (not business) account, posting in a supported region, with videos at least one minute long. This tool assumes you already meet those requirements — it's estimating per-video earnings, not eligibility.

Worth knowing: brand deals and TikTok Shop affiliate income routinely pay far more than the Rewards Program itself for creators with an engaged audience. This calculator only covers the Rewards Program specifically.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a "qualified view"?

A view that meets TikTok's internal watch-time and quality bar. Not every public view qualifies, which is why the number TikTok pays on is usually lower than what's publicly displayed on your video.

Do I need a certain follower count first?

Yes — Creator Rewards requires at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in the past 30 days, plus a personal (not business) account.

Why don't short videos qualify?

The program only pays out on videos at least one minute long. Anything shorter earns $0 from this specific program, even if it goes viral.

Is this estimate exact?

No. TikTok doesn't publish its exact formula. This gives a rough range based on commonly reported RPMs — your real payout also depends on originality, engagement, and search value, which aren't part of this calculation.
